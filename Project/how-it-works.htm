A clear path from need to scale

One mechanism. Better public outcomes.

We make innovation procurement easier to understand, easier to trust and easier to move forward.

The nine-stage pathway
01 / DEFINE

Departments frame the need.

Clear problem statements describe the outcome, context and constraints without prescribing a solution.

02 / TEST

Startups prove the fit.

Teams apply against a transparent brief and show the evidence behind their approach.

03 / SCALE

Everyone learns from the pilot.

Milestones and measurement turn a promising experiment into a procurement-ready pathway.

01

Government posts a challenge

A ministry or department identifies a real pain point or public problem and publishes the outcome, context, constraints and deadline.

02

Startups discover the challenge

Startups browse the public challenge view and decide whether the problem is relevant to their skills, sector and delivery capacity.

03

Startups apply

Teams check the eligibility, requirements, evaluation criteria and timeline before submitting their solution, team and evidence.

04

Complete sandbox or pilot testing

The selected startup tests its solution in a controlled government sandbox or live pilot, with agreed milestones, scope and success measures.

05

Government evaluation board reviews

An evaluation board reviews the pilot results against the published scorecard, evidence, public value, feasibility and delivery quality.

06

Periodic checks and officer reporting

Ministry officers schedule periodic checks and send notifications to the startup. Officers monitor milestones and maintain regular progress records.

07

Startups submit their work

Startups submit progress summaries, completed work, metrics, evidence and blockers for each reporting phase through the platform.

08

Ministry confirms satisfaction and pays

After the ministry confirms that the agreed work and outcomes are satisfactory, the applicable milestone or pilot payment is released to the startup.

09

Validation and next-stage decision

Results are validated using evidence and public outcomes. The government can recommend iteration, scale, procurement or a responsible close.
