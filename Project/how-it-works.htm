A clear path from need to scale

One mechanism. Better public outcomes.

We make innovation procurement easier to understand, easier to trust and easier to move forward.

The nine-stage pathway
01 / DEFINE

Departments frame the need.

Clear problem statements describe the outcome, context and constraints without prescribing a solution.

02 / TEST

Startups prove the fit.

Teams apply against a transparent brief and show the evidence behind their approach.

03 / SCALE

Everyone learns from the pilot.

Milestones and measurement turn a promising experiment into a procurement-ready pathway.

01

Government identifies & posts a challenge

A ministry or department identifies a real public problem and publishes the outcome, context, constraints, eligibility requirements and timeline.

02

Startups discover & assess the challenge

Startups browse challenges and assess whether the problem matches their capabilities, sector and delivery capacity.

03

Startups apply with their solution

Eligible startups submit their proposed solution, team details, evidence, implementation approach and required documents.

04

Government screens & evaluates applications

The department checks eligibility and evaluates submissions using the published evaluation criteria and scorecard. Shortlisted startups are selected for a pilot.

05

Pilot agreement is created & approved

The government and selected startup agree on the pilot scope, duration, milestones, deliverables, success measures, payment milestones, data and IP responsibilities, cybersecurity requirements, risks and responsibilities before the pilot begins.

06

Sandbox / pilot is conducted

The selected startup deploys and tests its solution in the agreed controlled environment or live setting according to the approved pilot agreement.

07

Monitoring, reporting & milestone verification

Government officers conduct periodic checks while startups submit progress updates, completed work, metrics, evidence and blockers. Milestones are verified against the pilot agreement.

08

Evaluation, validation & payment

The evaluation board reviews the pilot results and evidence against the agreed scorecard. Once applicable milestones are verified, the corresponding milestone or pilot payment is released.

09

Scale, procure, iterate or close

Based on validated results, the government decides whether to iterate or extend the pilot, scale the solution, move toward compliant procurement, or responsibly close the pilot.
